# Bench step 5 — the lost ACK, the critical case of ADR-0007.
# deafen makes the ground ignore everything it receives for 3 s, so the ACK
# of SET_RATE never reaches it. Expected, as on the UDP radio on 2026-09-19:
#   - ttcd goes to FAST (rate ... commanded) and, unconfirmed, back to
#     NOMINAL 20 s later (rate ... revert);
#   - the ground keeps retrying at NOMINAL until heard; ttcd changes again
#     and confirms (rate_commit);
#   - the ack of SET_RATE comes with attempts > 1 and ~21 s of latency, and
#     both ends finish on FAST.
deafen 3
rate fast
# The satellite commits the change once it hears the ground at FAST; the
# PINGs also show both ends did finish there.
ping 3
